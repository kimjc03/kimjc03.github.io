Physics · Research · Theory

Geometry, quantum matter,
and emergent structure.

I am an undergraduate researcher at Hanyang University with interests in quantum gravity and quantum many-body physics. I explore graph-theoretic descriptions of relativistic fields on discrete spacetime and study quantum dynamics in many-body lattice systems, including approaches based on quantum circuits.

Research interests

Current directions

A selection of topics that currently motivate my work. These are ongoing research directions, not claims of published results.

01 / Spacetime & field theory

Quantum Gravity

I explore graph-theoretic approaches to relativistic field theory on discrete spacetime, drawing on quantum field theory and discrete mathematics. My research focuses on discrete field equations, graph Laplacians, and their continuum limits, with potential implications for quantum gravity.

Relativistic Fields · Graph Theory
02 / Quantum matter

Quantum many-body physics

Transverse-field Ising models, time-dependent lattice growth, and many-body dynamics, including the use of quantum computers and quantum circuits to study lattice Hamiltonians.

Spin systems · Quantum dynamics
